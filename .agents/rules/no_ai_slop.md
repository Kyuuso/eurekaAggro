# Rule: No AI Slop Writing Directives

Adopted from https://github.com/realrossmanngroup/no_ai_slop_writing_rules

Whenever writing prose, documentation, commit messages, comments, or summaries:

## Non-Negotiable Directives

1. **No emdashes.** The character is banned. Use a semicolon, period, comma, parentheses, or restructure the sentence.
2. **No unsourced statistics or fabricated metrics.** Every number must be concrete and attributable.
3. **No parenthetical clarifications in headings.** Name what the section contains directly.
4. **No intensifiers.** Banned: "extremely", "dramatically", "exceptionally", "significantly", "incredibly", "remarkably", "truly", "absolutely", "literally". Prove it with a specific fact or delete the word.
5. **No hollow statements.** Every claim must end on a concrete, verifiable detail or fact.
6. **No repeated talking points.** Say it once; eliminate padding.
7. **Vary structure.** Break formulaic templates, repetitive sentence rhythms, and uniform list shapes.
8. **Reference without narrating the reference.** Do not write "as discussed above" or "as we will see."
9. **No performative urgency without concrete consequences.**
10. **No scare quotes on standard words.**
11. **No filler phrases.** Banned: "In today's world", "It's important to note", "When it comes to", "At the end of the day", "In the realm of", "It goes without saying", "This is where X comes in", "Look no further".
12. **Never start a sentence with "Whether you're."**
13. **Write like a technical researcher, not a marketing copywriter.** If a sentence could sit on a generic promotional page, replace it with technical specifics (names, IDs, packet structs, exact functions).
14. **No synthetic enthusiasm or cheerleader tone.** Avoid exclamation points, false hype, and cheerleading. State verifiable facts plainly.
15. **No weasel words.** Banned: "Helps ensure", "may be able to", "can potentially". Commit or cut.
16. **No narrative, dramatic, or teaser headings.** Headings state exactly what is in the section (e.g. "Packet parsing logic", not "Unveiling the Secrets of Instance Networking").
17. **No fabricated stories, milestones, or attributions.**
18. **No AI transition crutches.** Banned: "Furthermore", "Moreover", "Notwithstanding", "That being said", "At its core", "In essence", "It is worth noting that", "In the landscape of". Use plain connectors: also, and, but, however, still.
19. **No AI verbs.** Banned: delve, leverage, utilize, facilitate, foster, bolster, underscore, unveil, navigate (metaphorical), streamline. Use plain technical verbs: use, check, send, inspect, test, run, simplify.
20. **No academic or bureaucratic tells.** Banned: "shed light on", "pave the way for", "a myriad of", "a plethora of", "paramount", "pertaining to", "prior to" (use "before"), "subsequent to" (use "after").
